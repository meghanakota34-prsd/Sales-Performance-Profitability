# Sales Performance & Profitability Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze sales performance and profitability across different regions, product categories, and customer segments.

## Project Objective

To analyze sales, profit, order value, and product quantity, identify business trends, and support data-driven decision-making.

## Tools & Technologies

* Microsoft Power BI
* Power Query
* DAX
* Data Visualization

## Key Performance Indicators

* Total Sales: 12.64M
* Total Profit: 1.47M
* Average Order Value: 505.01
* Total Quantity: 178.31K

## Dashboard Features

* Monthly Sales Trend Analysis
* Sales by Product Category
* Profit by Region
* Sales by Customer Segment
* Top 10 Products by Sales
* Sales vs Profitability by Category
* Interactive Region, Date, and Category Filters

## Key Insights

* Technology recorded the highest sales among product categories.
* Consumer segment contributed the largest share of sales.
* Central region showed strong profitability.
* Monthly sales trends help monitor business performance.

## Skills Demonstrated

* Data Cleaning and Transformation
* Data Modeling
* DAX Calculations
* KPI Development
* Interactive Dashboard Design
* Business Intelligence

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Power BI transforms sales data into meaningful business insights, helping businesses monitor performance and understand profitability.
